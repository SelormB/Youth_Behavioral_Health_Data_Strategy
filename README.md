# Youth Behavioral Health Data Strategy

A shared, cross-district data strategy for youth behavioral health measurement across Estes Park, Poudre, and Thompson School Districts, developed during a Summer 2026 internship with the MHSU Alliance at the Health District of Northern Larimer County.

## Background

None of the three school districts in Larimer County currently share a common way of measuring student behavioral health. Each collects its own data, on its own schedule, using its own survey instruments — and none of them track school-level indicators like persistent sadness, suicidal ideation, or experiences of racism at all. This project builds the evidence base and the concrete recommendations needed to close that gap, without requiring any district to adopt anything it hasn't chosen to.

## How to use this package

- **New to the project?** Start with the Environmental Scan for the clearest picture of the local context and why this work matters.
- **Want the technical detail?** The Survey Crosswalk workbook shows exactly what each district measures today and how comparable it is across instruments.
- **Ready to act?** The Shared Metric Recommendations document has the specific, ready-to-use survey language.
- **Presenting or sharing externally?** The Stakeholder Guide and Deliverable 6 materials are built for that audience specifically — the internal presentation is not.

## Key terms

- **HKCS** — Healthy Kids Colorado Survey, the statewide biennial survey providing the verified baseline used throughout this project.
- **CO-MTSS** — Colorado Multi-Tiered System of Supports, the state framework these recommendations are designed to align with.
- **Disaggregated data** — results broken out by race, language, gender identity, and income rather than reported as a single average. This is a standing requirement across every recommendation in this project, not an optional add-on.
